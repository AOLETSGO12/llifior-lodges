Location & things to do

Llifior Lodges sit in a peaceful hamlet near Garthmyl, between Welshpool (6 miles) and Newtown (8 miles), with Berriew and Montgomery each about 3 miles away. The nearest pub is about 1.5 miles away and the nearest shop about 2.5 miles. The last stretch is a single-track drive with a steep gravel incline up to the lodges.

Out and about

Walking

Walks from the doorstep. Offa's Dyke Path is about 4.5 miles away, and the hills of Allt y Main and Y Golfa are about 12 miles away.

Cycling

National Cycle Route 81 passes nearby, and the towpath of the Montgomery Canal is an easy, flat ride. Secure bike storage is available at the lodges.

Castles & gardens

National Trust Powis Castle and Garden (Welshpool), Montgomery Castle, Dolforwyn Castle and Gregynog Hall and Gardens are all close by.

Steam railway

The Welshpool & Llanfair Light Railway runs heritage steam trains from Welshpool (6 miles) to Llanfair Caereinion.

Villages & towns

Berriew has independent pubs, restaurants and a deli. Montgomery has a Georgian square, and Newtown has museums, a modern art gallery and independent shops.

Further afield

Lake Vyrnwy, Pistyll Rhaeadr waterfall and the Shropshire Hills National Landscape all make good day trips.
